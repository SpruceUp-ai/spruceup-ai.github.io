SpruceUp

A standalone system for making automated, incremental updates to a vector database
Case Study View on GitHub

Incremental Indexing

Stop Embedding Rot Before It Starts

Vector databases degrade silently. As your knowledge base grows and changes, your old embeddings become stale, their semantic content no longer up-to-date with the documents they represent. Your app's retrieval quality quietly degrades without a single error or warning.

SpruceUp tracks your document sources and updates your vectors immediately and automatically as changes come in. Your vectors stay fresh. Your retrieval quality stays high.

Integration

Fits Into Existing Pipelines. Or Builds New Ones.

Already running a RAG application? SpruceUp slots in upstream of your vector store with minimal configuration. No rewrite required, no new infrastructure to manage.

Starting fresh? SpruceUp's connector model gives you a complete ingestion and sync layer out of the box, so you can focus on retrieval and generation from day one.

Flexibility

Your Stack, Your Rules

SpruceUp ships with connectors for the most popular embedders, document sources, and vector databases, with many more to come. Mix and match to fit your existing infrastructure. Swap a vector store without touching your embedding logic. Add a new source without rewiring the pipeline.

  • Embedders OpenAI · Cohere · Gemini · Voyage AI
  • Sources Filesystem · Google Drive
  • Vector Stores Pinecone · Weaviate · pgvector
